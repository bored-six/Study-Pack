# Mockup & Redesign Showcase Rules

When asked to provide a "showcase", "redesign", or "mockup" for the Flipp app:
- DO NOT provide just an isolated mobile screen mockup.
- ALWAYS build a comprehensive, high-fidelity documentation/ledger page (HTML artifact) that explains the mechanics, context, and UI details, similar to the "Achievement Ledger".
- ALWAYS rigorously adhere to the "School Derp" theme: lined paper backgrounds, chunky borders, pastel colors, hand-drawn/Patrick Hand typography, sticky notes, and tape.
- ALWAYS use 100% custom SVGs (no emojis) and include animations whenever possible.
